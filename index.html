01 — who I am

Curious first.
Builder second.

I'm Vishnu Tiwari, a student who is trying to become genuinely good at making things with technology.

Right now that means learning programming, mathematics, web development, AI/ML and robotics — not because I want to collect skills, but because I want enough understanding to turn ideas into real projects.

I learn best when there is something concrete to build. This website is part portfolio, part notebook, and part reminder of where I'm trying to go.

Things that keep me moving

  • ✦ Curiosity about how things actually work
  • ✦ Building instead of only watching tutorials
  • ✦ Mathematics for understanding, not just exams
  • ✦ AI, robotics and the space between software + hardware
  • ✦ The idea of eventually building something much bigger

02 — direction

I don't have the whole map.

My long-term aim is to become someone who can move comfortably between mathematics, software, AI, robotics and engineering. I want to keep increasing the difficulty of the problems I can solve and eventually work on ideas that have a real effect outside my laptop.
